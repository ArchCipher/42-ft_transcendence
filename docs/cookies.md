## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. They are widely used to make websites work, improve efficiency, and provide reporting information.

## How synctext uses cookies

synctext uses **one cookie only**, and it is strictly necessary for the service to function.

### Session cookie (essential)

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| **Name** | `session` (or equivalent session identifier) |
| **Purpose** | Keeps you signed in between page loads |
| **Type** | `HttpOnly`, `SameSite=Strict`, `Secure` |
| **Duration** | Expires after 7 days of inactivity |
| **Storage** | Server stores only the SHA-256 hash of the token. The raw value is in the cookie only |

This cookie is **essential** — without it, you would need to re-enter your credentials on every page. Because it is strictly necessary, no consent banner is required under the ePrivacy Directive.

## What we do NOT do

- We do **not** set advertising or tracking cookies.
- We do **not** use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any third-party analytics.
- We do **not** share cookie data with advertisers or third parties.
- We do **not** use cookies to build user profiles for marketing.

## Other tokens (not cookies)

Email verification and password reset tokens are sent to you via email as part of a link. They are not stored as cookies on your device. They are single-use and time-limited (24 hours for email verification, 30 minutes for password reset).

## Your choices

Since the only cookie we use is strictly necessary for the service, there is nothing to opt out of. If you disable cookies entirely in your browser, you will not be able to stay signed in to synctext.

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.
